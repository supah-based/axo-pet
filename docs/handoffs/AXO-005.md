## Operator intent and implemented result

Continue the existing Pet XP progression from 10 through 20 with increasingly expensive steps. Give fixed Gear item tiers 10–20 their own minimum equip levels and stronger stats, with no separate Gear XP. Add visually coordinated five-piece sets for brawler, guardian, skirmisher, arcanist, support and controller play styles. Completing five equipped pieces adds role stats and bounded attack, damage, shield, healing or purge bonuses. Mixed item tiers use the weakest piece as the set tier.

The source catalog contains 37 families, 407 unissued variants and 66 set-tier manifests. Bazaar examples and the fitting room use the expanded collection. Complete 2:3 NFT portraits and wearable surfaces remain separate. Fifteen new pose-first masters extend the fitted clothes/head/face/tool patterns; back, charm, companion and habitat items fill the remaining preview slots. Real lens alpha holes preserve every lineage's eyes. Appearance, selection and XP controls are previews, never verified ownership or XP awards.
