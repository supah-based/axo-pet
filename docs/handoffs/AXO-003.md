## Superseded fit

The user rejected the flat fit in `0626cdb`: rear vest lining and waistband interiors remained visible over skin, and tools had the wrong orientation and proportions. That bundle must not be treated as visually approved. V2 replaces its fitting approach within the existing renderer.

## Current implementation

The collection retains 30 designs across ten preview slots and six lineages. Standalone full-frame item art remains unchanged. The new pose-versioned rig defines neck, waist and grip landmarks. Item-local attachment origins control placement, mirroring and size. Rear-surface contours follow each garment's fitted reference transform; anatomical masks stay attached to the Pet.

All three vests and all three pants now have front and rear exports. Rear neckline/armhole lining and waistbands render below the Pet; front fabric, straps and belts render above it. Waist placement is raised to cover the original base clothing edge. Runesmith cuff interiors also have depth segmentation.

All three tools are mirrored and preserve their source proportions. Their handles register to the same grip anchor. Six lossless original-Pet finger projections draw over tools, and six arm projections draw over clothing. These are masked canonical pixels, not repainted anatomy. Missing compatible pose, garment depth or foreground assets fails the render rather than reverting to a flat overlay.

Studio includes an expandable **See how the gear fits** inspector for the nine affected designs, showing rear/front/worn views and optional attachment guides. Inspection does not change the outfit or saved PNG. The user's ten-piece Rose bookmark is preserved. Bazaar showcases regenerate through the same V2 plan.

This is an authored 2D rig for the fixed adult website pose. New designs require fitting contours and reviewed attachment points; new poses need their own rig. It does not automatically fit arbitrary art, drape cloth or animate a 3D character.
