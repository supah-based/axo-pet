08 / CLEAR ANSWERS

No mystery meat.
Just the model.

Is an AXO Pet already an NFT?+

The live Hatchery pet is wallet-bound in AXO's preserved ledger today. A soulbound ERC-721 and tradable Level-10 ERC-1155 gear system is built, but public minting remains gated until final art, IPFS, signer, deployment, and consent checks are complete.

Can I sell my AXO Pet?+

No. The pet is designed as permanent identity. Trust, XP, accuracy, history, rank, and access must never be sold. Level-10 gear is the transferable marketplace layer.

Does buying rare gear improve rewards?+

No. Gear may add only its capped stats inside supported games such as AXO Arena. It cannot add Hatchery XP, consensus weight, trust, access, Epoch rank, research authority, payouts, or financial return.

How do weekly rewards work?+

Verified epoch XP determines the Top 5 rank, using a 45/25/15/10/5 gross split. A projected pool is not the same as funded rewards; the interface labels that distinction and claims remain separately gated.

Does Crew popularity mean a contract is safe?+

No. Support count is a research-priority signal. It never overrides independent security, route, liquidity, or evidence checks and it does not enable live trading.
