Earn the equip level

Gear adds bounded Arena stats and set bonuses. Equip levels require earned Pet XP; equipment does not boost Hatchery XP, trust or rewards.

No identity flipping

The soul pet cannot transfer, approve, burn, relaunch, or carry a contributor's history into another wallet.

No pretend market

Zero listings means zero. Missing price means unknown. A catalog is not volume and rarity is not a return promise.

No custody shortcuts
